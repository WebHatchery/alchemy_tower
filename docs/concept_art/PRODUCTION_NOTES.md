# Concept-art production handoff

These studies are references for a future 2D art migration. They should not be
copied into runtime manifests as-is: they are painterly boards and mood pieces,
not native area plates, sprite sheets, or UI exports.

## Preserve

- Use old stone, worn timber, stained paper, aged brass, oxidised copper, and
  hand-blown glass as the shared material language.
- Keep teal alchemy light local to a tool, bottle, lamp, or station. Let value
  and silhouette carry readability before colour does.
- Show restoration as a change in the ground, beds, roads, water, and ordinary
  town activity. The tower is the instrument, not the beneficiary.
- Keep paths and work surfaces quiet enough for characters, markers, and touch
  prompts to read at actual game scale.
- Keep all player-facing text, quantities, labels, and state copy in the game.

## Review checklist

Before any study becomes production art, check it against the exact native
canvas in `docs/2d_asset_requirements.md`, then review it at in-game scale.

- Does the silhouette remain clear when reduced?
- Is the interaction area quieter than the surrounding decoration?
- Does a state change read through value, shape, or lighting as well as colour?
- Is the asset free of baked copy, labels, quantities, and UI instructions?
- Does the art still suggest tending and recovery rather than conquest?
